Master Percentages!

Percentages are everywhere - in sales, test scores, and daily life! "Percent" means "out of 100". This resource focuses on to understand, calculate, and use percentages!

Understanding a Percentage

A percentage shows a part out of 100. The % symbol means "out of 100".

Understanding Percent

50% = 50 out of 100 = 50/100 = 1/2 = 0.5

25% = 25 out of 100 = 25/100 = 1/4 = 0.25

100% = 100 out of 100 = the whole thing

0% = nothing

Real Life Examples:

• A test score of 80% means you got 80 out of 100 questions correct

• A sale with 25% off means you save 25 out of every 100 pence

• If 60% of students like pizza, that's 60 out of every 100 students

Let's Practice: Converting Between Percentages, Fractions, and Decimals

Percent to Fraction

Write the percent as a fraction with 100 as the denominator, then simplify

Examples:

• 50% = 50/100 = 1/2

• 25% = 25/100 = 1/4

• 75% = 75/100 = 3/4

• 20% = 20/100 = 1/5

Percent to Decimal

Divide by 100 (move decimal point 2 places left)

Examples:

• 50% = 50 ÷ 100 = 0.50 = 0.5

• 25% = 25 ÷ 100 = 0.25

• 5% = 5 ÷ 100 = 0.05

• 100% = 100 ÷ 100 = 1.0

Fraction to Percent

Convert to decimal first, then multiply by 100

Examples:

• 1/2 = 0.5 = 0.5 × 100 = 50%

• 1/4 = 0.25 = 0.25 × 100 = 25%

• 3/4 = 0.75 = 0.75 × 100 = 75%

Going Further: Finding a Percentage of and Amount

Method 1: Using Fractions

Example: Find 25% of 80

25% = 1/4

1/4 of 80 = 80 ÷ 4 = 20

Answer: 20

Method 2: Using Decimals

Example: Find 30% of 50

30% = 0.30

0.30 × 50 = 15

Answer: 15

Top Tips:
  • 10% = divide by 10 (easy to find!)
  • 50% = divide by 2 (half)
  • 25% = divide by 4 (quarter)
  • To find other percentages, find 10% first, then multiply
