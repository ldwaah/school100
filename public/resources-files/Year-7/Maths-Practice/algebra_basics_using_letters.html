Introduction to Algebra!

Algebra uses letters to represent numbers. This helps us solve problems and find unknown values. This resource focuses on to work with letters and solve simple equations!

Understanding Algebra

In algebra, we use letters (like x, y, a, b) to represent unknown numbers.

Using Letters

Instead of writing: "A number plus 5 equals 12"

We write: x + 5 = 12

The letter x represents the unknown number

Examples:

• x + 3 = 10 (x is the unknown number)

• y - 5 = 7 (y is the unknown number)

• 2a = 16 (a is the unknown number)

Let's Practice: Solving Simple Equations

To solve and equation, find the value of the letter that makes it true.

Example 1: x + 5 = 12

Step 1: What number plus 5 equals 12?

Step 2: 12 - 5 = 7

Answer: x = 7

Check: 7 + 5 = 12 ✓

Example 2: y - 3 = 8

Step 1: What number minus 3 equals 8?

Step 2: 8 + 3 = 11

Answer: y = 11

Check: 11 - 3 = 8 ✓

Example 3: 2a = 16

Step 1: What number times 2 equals 16?

Step 2: 16 ÷ 2 = 8

Answer: a = 8

Check: 2 × 8 = 16 ✓

Top Tips:
  • For addition: subtract to find the answer
  • For subtraction: add to find the answer
  • For multiplication: divide to find the answer
  • Always check your answer by substituting it back!
