Explore Angles and Shapes!

Angles and shapes are everywhere! This lesson will help you understand about different types of angles, how to identify shapes, and understand basic geometry!

Getting Started: Types of Angles

And angle is the space between two lines that meet at a point.

Right Angle

90° - exactly like the corner of a square or rectangle

Example: The corner of a book, the corner of a door

Acute Angle

Less than 90° - smaller than a right angle

Example: 30°, 45°, 60° are all acute angles

Obtuse Angle

More than 90° but less than 180° - bigger than a right angle

Example: 120°, 135°, 150° are all obtuse angles

Straight Angle

180° - a straight line

Example: A flat line

Let's Practice: Types of Triangles

A triangle has 3 sides and 3 angles. The angles always add up to 180°.

Equilateral Triangle

All 3 sides equal, all 3 angles equal (60° each)

Example: Sides: 5cm, 5cm, 5cm

Isosceles Triangle

2 sides equal, 2 angles equal

Example: Sides: 6cm, 6cm, 8cm

Scalene Triangle

All sides different, all angles different

Example: Sides: 3cm, 4cm, 5cm

Remember:
  • All triangles have 3 sides and 3 angles
  • Angles in a triangle always add up to 180°
  • Right-angled triangle has one 90° angle
  • Look at the sides to identify triangle types

Going Further: Your Practice Tasks

Task 1: Identifying Angles
For each angle, say if it's acute, right, obtuse, or straight:

a) 45°
b) 90°
c) 120°
d) 180°
e) 30°
f) 150°
Task 2: Triangle Types
Identify each triangle type (equilateral, isosceles, or scalene):

a) Triangle with sides 5cm, 5cm, 5cm
b) Triangle with sides 6cm, 6cm, 8cm
c) Triangle with sides 3cm, 4cm, 5cm
d) Triangle with sides 7cm, 7cm, 7cm
e) Triangle with sides 4cm, 5cm, 6cm
Task 3: Angle Calculations
In a triangle, the angles add up to 180°. Find the missing angle:

a) Triangle with angles 60° and 60°. What is the third angle?
b) Triangle with angles 90° and 45°. What is the third angle?
c) Triangle with angles 50° and 70°. What is the third angle?
d) Triangle with angles 30° and 80°. What is the third angle?
Task 4: Word Problems
Solve these word problems. Show your working:

a) A triangle has angles of 60°, 60°, and 60°. What type of triangle is it?
b) If one angle is 90°, what type of angle is it?
c) A triangle has two angles of 45° each. What is the third angle?
d) A triangle has sides 8cm, 8cm, and 10cm. What type of triangle is it?
