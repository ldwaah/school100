Creative Writing Challenge: Complete Story

Year 7 English - Writing 60 minutes Full Story Writing

Put All Your Skills Together!

This is your chance to create a complete story using everything you've learned about creative writing! You'll plan, write, and edit a full narrative that showcases descriptive language, dialogue, varied sentences, proper paragraphing, and an engaging plot. Let your imagination shine!

Getting Started: Reminder of Story Elements

Story Structure

  • Beginning: Introduce characters, setting, hook the reader
  • Build-up: Develop the situation, create tension
  • Problem/Climax: The main conflict or exciting moment
  • Resolution: How the problem is solved
  • Ending: Wrap up, leave a lasting impression
Writing Skills to Include:
  • Descriptive language (five senses)
  • Show, don't tell
  • Varied sentence types and lengths
  • Dialogue with correct punctuation
  • Clear paragraphing (TiPToP)
  • Strong opening technique
  • Interesting vocabulary

Let's Practice: Choose Your Story Prompt

Select ONE of these prompts to write your story:

Option 1: The Unexpected Discovery

Whilst exploring somewhere familiar (your garden, local park, school, etc.), you discover something that shouldn't be there. What is it? What happens next?

Option 2: The Storm

You're somewhere safe when the biggest storm in decades hits. Power cuts, flooding, chaos. How do you and those around you cope? What happens?

Option 3: The Mix-Up

Someone mistakes you for someone else - a celebrity, their friend, or someone important. You decide to go along with it. What happens?

Option 4: The Competition

You enter a competition you're determined to win (sports, talent show, gaming, etc.). You face challenges and rivals. What happens?

Option 5: Your Own Idea

Create your own story idea! It must have: a main character, a problem or challenge, and a resolution.

Going Further: Planning Your Story

Task 1: Story Plan
Plan your story using this structure. Write brief notes for each section:

Chosen Prompt: [Which one?]

Main Character(s): [Names, ages, key traits]

Setting: [Where and when]

Beginning: [How will you start?]

Build-up: [What happens to create tension?]

Problem/Climax: [The main event]

Resolution: [How is it solved?]

Ending: [How will you finish?]

Final Challenge: Write Your Complete Story

Success Criteria - Your Story Must Include:

  • Clear structure (beginning, middle, end)
  • At least 250 words (aim for 300-400)
  • Descriptive language (at least 2 senses)
  • At least one piece of dialogue
  • Varied sentence types (simple, compound, complex)
  • Clear paragraphs with TiPToP breaks
  • Interesting vocabulary (avoid boring words)
  • A satisfying ending
Task 2: Your Complete Story
Write your full story here. Take your time. Remember to show, don't tell. Make it engaging!

Minimum: 250 words | Recommended: 300-400 words

Edit and Reflect

Task 3: Self-Assessment
Review your story using COPS (Capitals, Organisation, Punctuation, Spelling). List:

a) THREE things you're proud of in your story
b) TWO areas you could improve
c) Any changes you made whilst editing
Task 4: Techniques Used
Identify and list the writing techniques you included in your story. For each one, give a specific example from your story.

Example: "Descriptive language - 'The rain hammered against the windows like tiny fists'"

List at least 5 techniques with examples.
Task 5: Story Title
What title will you give your story? Choose something interesting and relevant that would make people want to read it. Explain why you chose this title.

Final Reflection

Reflection:
Think about all the Year 7 writing skills you've practised this year: descriptive writing, story openings, dialogue, paragraphing, persuasive writing, sentence variety, editing, etc. Which skills do you feel most confident with now? Which would you like to practise more? What have you learned about being a good writer?
