Character Analysis: Understanding People in Stories

Year 7 English - Reading 60 minutes Character Development

Meet the Characters!

Characters are the people in stories. When we read, we can learn a lot about them by paying attention to what they say, what they do, and how they treat others. Today, you'll become a character detective!

Understanding Characters

To understand a character, we ask three simple questions:

1. What do they DO?

Look at the character's actions and behaviour.

  • Are they kind or mean?
  • Do they help people or ignore them?
  • Are they brave or scared?

2. What do they SAY?

Listen to how the character talks.

  • Are they polite or rude?
  • Do they speak kindly or harshly?
  • What words do they use?

3. How do OTHERS react to them?

See how other characters feel about them.

  • Do people like them?
  • Do others smile or frown around them?
  • Do people want to be near them or avoid them?
Top Tips for Understanding Characters:
  • Watch what characters do, not just what they say
  • Look for clues about their feelings
  • Think about why they behave the way they do
  • Find examples from the story to prove your ideas
Example:

"Emma saw the new student sitting alone at lunch. She walked over with a big smile. 'Hi! I'm Emma. Would you like to sit with me and my friends?'"

What does this tell us about Emma?

Friendly – She smiles and talks to the new student

Kind – She doesn't want anyone to sit alone

Welcoming – She invites the new student to join her group

Question 1:
What kind of person is Mia? Write down three words that describe her personality.
Question 2:
Find two examples from the story that show Mia is kind. Write out what she does.
Question 3:
How does Tyler behave at the party? Give two examples of things he does or says.
Question 4:
What kind of person is Tyler? Write down two words that describe him and explain why.
Question 5:
How does Mia help Oliver feel comfortable at the party? What does she do?
Question 6:
What does Oliver say about Mia at the end? Why does he feel this way?
