Anatomy & Movement: How Your Body Works

Year 7 Sport - Academic 60 minutes Body & Movement

Understanding Your Body!

Your body is amazing! It has bones, muscles, and joints that work together to help you move. You'll explore about how your body works when you play sport, what muscles do, and how to keep your body healthy and strong!

Getting Started: Your Skeleton and Bones

Your skeleton is made up of bones that give your body shape and support. You have over 200 bones in your body!

Main Bones in Sport:

• Skull: Protects your brain

• Spine: Your backbone - keeps you upright

• Ribs: Protect your heart and lungs

• Arms and Legs: Help you move and play

Why Bones Matter in Sport:

Bones provide structure so you can run, jump, and throw. Strong bones help prevent injuries when you play sport.

Key Points About Bones:
  • Bones give your body shape and support
  • Strong bones come from exercise and good nutrition
  • Bones protect important organs like your brain and heart
  • Different bones help with different movements

Let's Practice: Muscles and How They Work

Muscles are what make your body move. You have over 600 muscles!

Types of Muscles:

• Voluntary muscles: You control these - like your arm and leg muscles

• Involuntary muscles: Work automatically - like your heart

How Muscles Work:

Muscles work in pairs. When one muscle contracts (gets shorter), the other relaxes (gets longer). This creates movement!

Example: To bend your arm, your bicep contracts and your tricep relaxes. To straighten it, the opposite happens!

Muscles Used in Sport:

• Running: Leg muscles (quadriceps, hamstrings, calves)

• Throwing: Arm and shoulder muscles

• Jumping: Leg muscles and core muscles

Going Further: Joints and Movement

Joints are where two bones meet. They allow your body to bend and move!

Types of Joints:

• Hinge joints: Like your knee and elbow - bend in one direction (like a door hinge)

• Ball and socket joints: Like your shoulder and hip - can move in many directions

• Pivot joints: Like your neck - allows rotation

How Joints Help in Sport:

Different joints allow different movements. Your shoulder joint lets you throw a ball in many directions. Your knee joint lets you run and jump!

Keeping Your Body Healthy:
  • Warm up before exercise to prepare your muscles and joints
  • Stretch after exercise to keep muscles flexible
  • Eat healthy food to build strong bones and muscles
  • Drink water to keep your body working properly
