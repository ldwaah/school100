History of Sports: How Sports Began

Year 7 Sport - Academic 60 minutes Sports History

Discover Sports History!

Sports have been around for thousands of years! This resource focuses on about where sports came from, how they've changed over time, and why people have always loved to compete and play games!

Getting Started: Ancient Sports

People have been playing sports for thousands of years! The ancient Greeks started the Olympic Games over 2,700 years ago.

Ancient Olympic Games:

The first Olympics were held in Greece in 776 BC. They included running, jumping, wrestling, and throwing events. Only men could compete, and winners received olive wreaths!

Why Sports Started:

People played sports to:

• Stay fit and healthy

• Train for battle

• Have fun and compete

• Honour the gods

Key Points About Sports History:
  • Sports have been important for thousands of years
  • Many modern sports come from ancient games
  • Sports have changed and improved over time
  • Today, sports bring people from all over the world together

Let's Practice: How Sports Have Changed

Sports have changed a lot over the years. Let's see how:

Football History:

Modern football started in England in the 1800s. Before that, people played similar games with different rules. The Football Association was formed in 1863 to create standard rules.

Basketball History:

Basketball was invented in 1891 by Dr James Naismith in America. He wanted a game that could be played indoors during winter. The first game used peach baskets as hoops!

Modern Olympics:

The modern Olympic Games started in 1896. Now they happen every 4 years and include many more sports. Both men and women can compete, and athletes come from all over the world!

Going Further: Famous Moments in Sports History

Throughout history, there have been amazing moments in sport:

Breaking Barriers:

In 1960, Abebe Bikila from Ethiopia won the Olympic marathon running barefoot! He was the first African to win and Olympic gold medal.

Women in Sport:

Women weren't allowed in the first modern Olympics in 1896. Now, women compete in almost all sports and have achieved amazing things!
