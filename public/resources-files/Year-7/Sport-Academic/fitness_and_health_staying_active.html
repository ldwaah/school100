Staying Fit and Healthy!

Being active and fit helps you feel good, play better, and stay healthy! You'll explore about different types of fitness, why exercise is important, and how to stay active in fun ways!

Understanding Fitness

Fitness means being able to do physical activities without getting too tired. There are different types of fitness:

1. Cardiovascular Fitness (Cardio):

This is about your heart and lungs. Activities like running, swimming, and cycling make your heart stronger and help you breathe better.

2. Strength:

This is about how strong your muscles are. Activities like climbing, lifting, and pushing make your muscles stronger.

3. Flexibility:

This is about how bendy you are. Stretching and yoga help you move more easily and prevent injuries.

4. Endurance:

This is about how long you can keep going. Activities like long-distance running or cycling build endurance.

Key Points About Fitness:
  • Different activities build different types of fitness
  • Being fit helps you play sport better
  • Fitness makes you feel good and gives you energy
  • You can improve your fitness by practising regularly

Let's Practice: Why Exercise is Important

Exercise has many benefits for your body and mind:

Physical Benefits:

• Makes your heart and lungs stronger

• Builds strong bones and muscles

• Helps you maintain a healthy weight

• Gives you more energy

• Helps you sleep better

Mental Benefits:

• Makes you feel happier

• Reduces stress and worry

• Boosts your confidence

• Helps you concentrate better

• Makes you feel proud of what you can do

How Much Exercise?

Experts recommend at least 60 minutes of physical activity every day. This can be playing sport, walking, cycling, dancing, or any activity that gets you moving!

Going Further: Building Your Fitness

You can improve your fitness by doing different activities regularly:

Ways to Build Fitness:

• Play sport: Football, basketball, netball, tennis - any sport you enjoy!

• Walk or cycle: Instead of being driven, walk or cycle places

• Dance: Dancing is great exercise and fun!

• Active games: Tag, hide and seek, playground games

• Help at home: Gardening, cleaning, walking the dog

Setting Goals:

Set small goals to improve your fitness. For example:

• "I'll run for 5 minutes without stopping"

• "I'll do 10 push-ups"

• "I'll walk to school twice this week"

When you reach a goal, set a new one!

Staying Motivated:
  • Choose activities you enjoy
  • Exercise with friends
  • Set small, achievable goals
  • Celebrate your progress
  • Remember how good you feel after exercise
