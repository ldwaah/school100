Nutrition for Athletes: Fueling Your Body

Year 7 Sport - Academic 60 minutes Nutrition

Fuel Your Body Right!

Just like a car needs fuel to run, your body needs the right food to play sport well! In this resource, you'll discover about what foods give you energy, what helps you build strong muscles, and how to eat well for sport!

Getting Started: Food Groups and What They Do

Different foods do different jobs in your body. Let's learn about the main food groups:

1. Carbohydrates (Carbs):

These give you energy! Foods like bread, pasta, rice, potatoes, and fruit give you the energy you need to run, jump, and play.

Best for: Before and after exercise

2. Protein:

These help build and repair muscles! Foods like chicken, fish, eggs, beans, and nuts help your muscles grow strong.

Best for: After exercise to help muscles recover

3. Fruits and Vegetables:

These give you vitamins and minerals! They help your body work properly and stay healthy.

Best for: Every day - try to eat 5 portions!

4. Dairy:

These help build strong bones! Milk, cheese, and yogurt give you calcium for strong bones.

Best for: Every day for growing bones

Key Points About Nutrition:
  • Eat a variety of foods from all food groups
  • Carbs give you energy to play
  • Protein helps build strong muscles
  • Fruits and vegetables keep you healthy
  • Drink plenty of water!

Let's Practice: Eating for Sport

What and when you eat can help you play better:

Before Exercise:

Eat carbs 1-2 hours before playing sport. This gives you energy!

Good choices: Banana, toast, pasta, cereal

Avoid: Heavy, fatty foods that make you feel slow

During Exercise:

Drink water! Your body needs water when you're active.

For activities longer than 1 hour, you might need a small snack like a banana.

After Exercise:

Eat protein and carbs within 30 minutes to 2 hours after exercise.

Good choices: Chicken and rice, milk and banana, eggs on toast

This helps your muscles recover and get stronger!

Going Further: Healthy Eating Habits

Good eating habits help you stay healthy and play better:

Breakfast:

Breakfast is important! It gives you energy to start the day. Good breakfasts include cereal, toast, fruit, and milk.

Regular Meals:

Eat regular meals throughout the day. Don't skip meals - your body needs fuel!

Snacks:

Healthy snacks between meals can give you energy. Good choices: fruit, yogurt, nuts, or a small sandwich.

Treats:

Sweets, crisps, and fizzy drinks are okay sometimes, but not every day. They don't give you the energy you need for sport!

Top Tips for Healthy Eating:
  • Eat breakfast every day
  • Eat a variety of foods
  • Drink plenty of water
  • Eat regular meals
  • Save treats for special occasions
