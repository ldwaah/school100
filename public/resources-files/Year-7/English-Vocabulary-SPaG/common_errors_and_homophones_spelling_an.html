Common Errors & Homophones: Spelling & Word Confusion

Year 7 English - Vocabulary & SPaG 60 minutes Fixing Mistakes

Avoid Common Mistakes!

Some words sound the same but are spelled differently (homophones), and some words are commonly confused. Learn to spot and fix these errors to make your writing clearer and more professional!

Getting Started: Homophones - Words That Sound Alike

Homophones are words that sound the same but have different meanings and spellings.

Common Homophones:

their / there / they're

• their = belonging to them → "Their books are here."

• there = in that place → "The shop is over there."

• they're = they are → "They're happy today."

your / you're

• your = belonging to you → "Is this your coat?"

• you're = you are → "You're my friend."

its / it's

• its = belonging to it → "The dog wagged its tail."

• it's = it is → "It's a sunny day."

to / too / two

• to = going towards → "I went to school."

• too = also / very → "I like pizza too." / "It's too hot."

• two = the number 2 → "I have two cats."

where / were / we're / wear

• where = what place → "Where are you going?"

• were = past tense of "are" → "We were happy."

• we're = we are → "We're going out."

• wear = to have clothes on → "I wear a uniform."

here / hear

• here = this place → "Come here!"

• hear = to listen → "Can you hear me?"

Wrong:

"Their going to there house." (should be "They're going to their house.")

"Your late for you're lesson." (should be "You're late for your lesson.")

Correct:

"They're going to their house."

"You're late for your lesson."

Let's Practice: Common Spelling Mistakes

Words Often Spelled Wrong:

definitely (not "definately" or "defiantly")

separate (not "seperate")

necessary (not "neccessary")

receive (not "recieve") - Remember: "i before e except after c"

because (not "becuase")

beautiful (not "beutiful")

library (not "libary")

Wednesday (not "Wendsday")

Spelling Tips:
  • Break words into syllables: sep-a-rate, li-bra-ry
  • Learn tricky parts: def-in-it-ely
  • Remember rules: "i before e except after c" (receive, believe)
  • Practice writing the word several times

Going Further: Commonly Confused Words

affect / effect

• affect = to influence (verb) → "The rain will affect the match."

• effect = a result (noun) → "The rain had an effect on the match."

lose / loose

• lose = to misplace / not win → "Don't lose your keys." / "We might lose the game."

• loose = not tight → "My shoe is loose."

then / than

• then = next / at that time → "First we eat, then we play."

• than = comparing → "She is taller than me."

who's / whose

• who's = who is → "Who's coming to the party?"

• whose = belonging to who → "Whose book is this?"
