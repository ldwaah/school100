Pronouns: Using Them Correctly

Year 7 English - Vocabulary & SPaG 60 minutes Pronouns

Replace Nouns with Pronouns!

Pronouns are words that replace nouns to avoid repetition. Instead of saying "Tom went to Tom's house," we say "Tom went to his house." Learn to use pronouns correctly to make your writing clearer and more elegant!

Understanding a Pronoun

A pronoun replaces a noun to avoid repeating the same word.

Without pronouns (repetitive):

"Tom went to Tom's house. Tom's mum made Tom a sandwich."

With pronouns (better):

"Tom went to his house. His mum made him a sandwich."

Let's Practice: Types of Pronouns

Personal Pronouns (Subject)

Replace the subject of a sentence: I, you, he, she, it, we, they

Examples:

• "I went to the shop."

• "She loves reading."

• "They are my friends."

Personal Pronouns (Object)

Replace the object: me, you, him, her, it, us, them

Examples:

• "The teacher helped me."

• "I saw her yesterday."

• "Give it to them."

Possessive Pronouns

Show ownership: mine, yours, his, hers, its, ours, theirs

Examples:

• "This book is mine."

• "That bag is yours."

• "The red car is theirs."

Possessive Adjectives

Come before nouns: my, your, his, her, its, our, their

Examples:

• "My book is on the table."

• "Your bag is here."

• "their house is big."

Going Further: Common Mistakes

1. Using "I" vs "me"

Wrong:

"Me and Tom went shopping." (should be "Tom and I")

"The teacher gave it to Tom and I." (should be "Tom and me")

Correct:

Use "I" as the subject: "Tom and I went shopping."

Use "me" as the object: "The teacher gave it to Tom and me."

Tip: Remove the other person to check:
  • "Me went shopping" sounds wrong → "I went shopping" ✓
  • "The teacher gave it to I" sounds wrong → "The teacher gave it to me" ✓

2. Vague Pronouns

Wrong (unclear):

"Tom and Jake went shopping. He bought trainers." (Who is "he"?)

Correct (clear):

"Tom and Jake went shopping. Tom bought trainers."

OR "Tom and Jake went shopping. Jake bought trainers."

Final Challenge: Your Practice Tasks

Task 1: Replace with Pronouns
Rewrite these sentences using pronouns to avoid repetition:

a) Sarah went to Sarah's house. Sarah's mum made Sarah a cake.
b) The students completed the students' homework. The students handed the homework to the teacher.
c) Tom found Tom's keys. Tom's keys were under Tom's bed.
Task 2: Choose I or Me
Choose the correct pronoun:

a) Sarah and (I/me) went to the cinema.
b) The teacher helped Tom and (I/me).
c) (I/Me) and my friend love reading.
d) Can you give it to Jake and (I/me)?
Task 3: Fix Vague Pronouns
These sentences have vague pronouns. Rewrite them to make them clear:

a) Emma and Mia went shopping. She bought a dress.
b) The cat chased the dog. It ran away quickly.
c) Tom gave Jake his book. He was happy.
Task 4: Identify Pronoun Types
In this sentence, identify which pronouns are used and what type they are:

"I gave my book to her because hers was lost."

Label each: I = ?, my = ?, her = ?, hers = ?
Task 5: Fix All Pronoun Errors
This paragraph has pronoun errors. Rewrite it correctly:

"Me and Sarah went to the library. Sarah found a book that Sarah wanted. Sarah showed the book to me and I. The book was hers, so Sarah took the book home."
Task 6: Write Your Own
Write THREE sentences using pronouns. Each sentence should use a different type:
• Sentence 1: Use a subject pronoun (I, you, he, she, etc.)
• Sentence 2: Use and object pronoun (me, you, him, her, etc.)
• Sentence 3: Use a possessive pronoun (mine, yours, his, hers, etc.)
