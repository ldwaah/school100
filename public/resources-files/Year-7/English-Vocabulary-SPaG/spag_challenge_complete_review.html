SPaG Challenge: Complete Review

Year 7 English - Vocabulary & SPaG 60 minutes Complete Skills Test

Put All Your SPaG Skills Together!

This is your chance to show everything you've learned about Spelling, Punctuation, and Grammar! You'll test your knowledge of parts of speech, punctuation, tenses, pronouns, connectives, and more. Good luck!

Getting Started: Parts of Speech Challenge

Task 1: Identify Word Types
In this sentence, identify each word as Noun (N), Verb (V), Adjective (Adj), or Adverb (Adv):

"The quick brown fox jumps quickly over the lazy dog."

Label each word with its type.

Let's Practice: Punctuation Mastery

Task 2: Fix All Punctuation
This paragraph has multiple punctuation errors (commas, apostrophes, speech marks, colons, semicolons). Rewrite it correctly:

"me and sarah went to london yesterday it was definitely a good trip we saw big been buckingham palace and the tower of london sarahs favourite was the tower she said its amazing we're going back next year"

Going Further: Grammar Essentials

Task 3: Fix Grammar Errors
This paragraph has multiple grammar errors (subject-verb agreement, tenses, pronouns, sentence structure). Rewrite it correctly:

"The students was excited about the school trip. Last week we goes to london and we see lots of interesting things. me and my friend loves museums. The museums was amazing. we had fun and we is tired when we gets home."

Final Challenge: Homophones & Spelling

Task 4: Choose Correct Words
Rewrite these sentences choosing the correct homophones and spelling:

a) (their/There/They're) going to (there/their/they're) house because (its/it's) (there/their/they're) birthday.
b) (Your/You're) late! (Your/You're) book is over (their/there/they're).
c) I wanted to (lose/lose) weight but my belt was (to/too/two) (lose/lose).
d) It's a (definitely/definitely) good idea to check (your/you're) spelling.

Connectives & Tenses

Task 5: Add Connectives & Fix Tenses
Rewrite this paragraph, adding appropriate connectives and fixing all tense errors:

"I wake up yesterday. I get dressed. I eat breakfast. I want to go out but it rain. I decide to stay inside. I finish my homework. I feel good."

Complete Paragraph Challenge

Task 6: Write a Perfect Paragraph
Write a paragraph (100-120 words) about your favourite subject at school. Make sure it includes:
• Correct spelling and punctuation
• A mix of sentence types (simple, compound, complex)
• At least 3 different connectives
• Consistent past or present tense
• Proper use of pronouns
• No grammar errors

Vocabulary Challenge

Task 7: Word Building
Break down these words into their parts and explain what they mean:

a) unhelpful
b) disappearance
c) carefully
d) misunderstanding

For each word, identify: prefix (if any), root word, suffix (if any), and what the whole word means.
