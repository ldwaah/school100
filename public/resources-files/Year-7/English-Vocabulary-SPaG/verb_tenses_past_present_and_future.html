Verb Tenses: Past, Present & Future

Year 7 English - Vocabulary & SPaG 60 minutes Verb Tenses

Master Verb Tenses!

Verb tenses tell us WHEN something happened, is happening, or will happen. Using the correct tense makes your writing clear and accurate. Today, you'll master past, present, and future tenses!

Getting Started: Present Tense

The present tense describes actions happening now or things that are always true.

Simple Present

I/you/we/they: walk, play, study

he/she/it: walks, plays, studies (add -s or -es)

Examples:

• "I walk to school every day."

• "She loves reading."

• "The sun rises in the east." (always true)

Present Continuous (happening now)

am/is/are + verb + -ing

Examples:

• "I am reading a book." (right now)

• "She is playing football."

• "They are studying for their test."

Let's Practice: Past Tense

The past tense describes actions that already happened.

Simple Past

Regular verbs: add -ed (walked, played, studied)

Irregular verbs: change completely (went, saw, came, did, was)

Examples:

• "Yesterday, I walked to school."

• "She went to the shop."

• "We saw a film last night."

Past Continuous (was happening)

was/were + verb + -ing

Examples:

• "I was reading when the phone rang."

• "They were playing when it started raining."

Common Irregular Past Tense Verbs:
  • go → went, see → saw, come → came, do → did, be → was/were
  • have → had, make → made, take → took, give → gave
  • say → said, tell → told, think → thought, know → knew

Going Further: Future Tense

The future tense describes actions that will happen.

Simple Future

will + verb OR am/is/are + going to + verb

Examples:

• "Tomorrow, I will visit my friend."

• "She is going to play football."

• "They will finish their homework."

Final Challenge: Keeping Tenses Consistent

Use the same tense throughout a sentence or paragraph unless you're showing time changes.

Wrong (mixed tenses):

"Yesterday I walk to school and I see my friend." (past mixed with present)

"Last week we goes to London and we see lots of things." (wrong verb forms)

Correct (consistent tense):

"Yesterday I walked to school and I saw my friend."

"Last week we went to London and we saw lots of things."

Your Practice Tasks

Task 1: Identify the Tense
Identify whether each sentence is past, present, or future tense:

a) I walked to school yesterday.
b) She is reading a book now.
c) Tomorrow I will visit my friend.
d) The sun rises every morning.
e) They were playing when I arrived.
Task 2: Change to Past Tense
Change these present tense sentences to past tense:

a) I walk to school every day.
b) She plays football on Saturdays.
c) They study in the library.
d) He goes to the shop.
Task 3: Change to Future Tense
Change these sentences to future tense:

a) I visit my friend.
b) She finishes her homework.
c) They play in the park.
d) We go to the cinema.
Task 4: Fix Mixed Tenses
These sentences mix tenses incorrectly. Fix them:

a) Yesterday I go to the shop and I buy some milk.
b) Last week we goes to London and we see lots of things.
c) Tomorrow I will walk to school and I see my friends.
Task 5: Write in Different Tenses
Write THREE sentences about the same activity (e.g., reading, playing football, going shopping):
• Sentence 1: Past tense (what you did)
• Sentence 2: Present tense (what you do now)
• Sentence 3: Future tense (what you will do)
Task 6: Irregular Verbs
Write the past tense of these irregular verbs:

a) go
b) see
c) do
d) make
e) take
f) give
g) come
h) be (I/she/he = ? We/you/they = ?)
